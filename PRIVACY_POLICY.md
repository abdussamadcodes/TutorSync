# TutorSync Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 7, 2026**

TutorSync is an offline-first Android application designed to help private tutors manage students, subjects, schedules, attendance, and payments.

## 1. Information We Handle

TutorSync may handle the following information:

- Student names and contact information.
- Subject and enrollment details.
- Attendance and lesson records.
- Schedule and reminder information.
- Fee agreements, invoices, and payment records.
- Account authentication information.

Users are responsible for ensuring they have permission to enter and manage information about their students.

## 2. Local Data Storage

TutorSync stores application records locally on the user's device. These records are not automatically uploaded to our servers.

Users are responsible for protecting their devices and maintaining appropriate backups.

## 3. Google Account and Authentication

TutorSync may use Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication to authenticate users.

Google account information is used for authentication and account-related functionality. TutorSync does not request access to unrelated Google Drive files.

## 4. Google Drive Backup

If users choose to enable Google Drive backup:

- TutorSync stores backup data in the application's private Google Drive AppData folder.
- The backup is associated with the Google account selected by the user.
- TutorSync uses the Google Drive `drive.appdata` permission.
- Other applications cannot normally access this private application data through Google Drive.
- Users can choose whether to use the backup feature.

## 5. Data Sharing

TutorSync does not sell personal information.

Information is not shared with advertisers. Data may be processed by Google services when users choose authentication or backup features.

## 6. Data Security

TutorSync uses local storage and supported Google authentication and backup mechanisms to help protect user information.

Users should keep their devices and Google accounts secure.

## 7. Data Deletion

Users can delete locally stored application data through the available application functions or by removing the application.

Google Drive backup data should be deleted through the application's backup-management functionality when available.

Deleting the application does not necessarily delete an existing cloud backup.

## 8. Children's and Student Information

TutorSync is designed for tutors managing their teaching activities. Users are responsible for obtaining any necessary permission before entering student information.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when TutorSync's features or data practices change.
